"By The Numbers" treats 19 November 2026 as a dashboard rather than a front page. Six oversized figures carry the whole day: 13 years of waiting ending with the global launch of GTA VI, a Van Gogh selling for a record $190.4 million, a proposed EUR 45 billion EU loan for Ukraine, Voyager 1 reaching one light-day from Earth, more than 11,000 Ebola cases in DR Congo, and Anthropic heading toward a $1.4 trillion market debut. Each number is set large enough to read across a room, with a short caption underneath doing the explaining.

The layout is deliberately flat and unsentimental, because the contrast is the story. A spacecraft's quiet milestone sits in the same grid as an auction record and an outbreak count, and none of them is allowed to shout louder than the others. If you only remember the figures, you have still understood what kind of day 19 November 2026 was.
